青岚境 / Earth 2 Xianxia — material provenance
===========================================
This is an interpreted, fictional walkable landscape, NOT a digital twin of a real hiking route. Route layout, camera, placement, collisions and cave arrangement are authored. Close terrain uses scanned rocks and photographically derived gentle ground; the distant band uses a credited Huangshan photograph, not reconstructed elevations or walkable geometry. The cave body and entrances use the licensed real captures credited below, adapted and joined for this fictional journey. Visual acceptance is still pending.

Close-scale photographed landscape
----------------------------------
The mountain-only entry now uses Coastal Cliff 04 by Poly Haven (CC0):
https://polyhaven.com/a/coastal_cliff_04
Native-scale scan surfaces are joined offline to a graded walking corridor
derived from the captured height profile and Rocky Trail photographic
displacement. Revision 3 infill uses isotropically filtered captured support;
its generated triangles remain below 45 degrees. Cliff silhouettes, ledges
and overhangs retain the scan's native geometry, without backing shells or
lateral planar caps.
Four shared near/far tiles repeat with alternating X reflection.
The two one-metre terminal bands blend the tangent between reflected blocks;
the remaining captured interior is not enlarged. The visible baked triangles
also provide foot height and wall collision. This is an authored composite,
not a continuous one-kilometre survey. No aerial geometry is loaded by this entry.
The earlier aerial terrain described below is retained only for legacy code.

Mountain walking surfaces use Forest Floor by Poly Haven, CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor
Bare-rock detail uses Rock043L by ambientCG, CC0:
https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock043L
Full photographic layers repeat at 2.14m and 1.8m; native detail crops at .2675m
and .225m add grain. Original albedo, normal and roughness are repacked into
precompressed GPU texture blocks; source dimensions, crop bounds, format,
licenses and checksums are listed in surface/manifest.json. The complete
forest-floor photograph is offset and quarter-turned in overlapping patches,
with matching normal and roughness samples; its original colors are retained.
The native crop only contributes grain, not the leaf distribution. Scattered
rocks retain their captured scale. The four captured fern variants form
irregular mixed-age patches with size factors of 0.55–2.4; their scaled root
footprints are grounded against the same visible tiles. Low/High use at most
190/350 placement candidates per native phase, before support/path rejection.
Entry vegetation includes native-scale Pine Sapling Small by Poly Haven, CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_small
Only one original approximately 1.05m seedling is retained, with quantized
geometry and compressed photographic albedo/alpha; it is not enlarged.
The taller pines use an original approximately 7.01m Pine Sapling Medium model:
https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_medium
Its native three-dimensional trunk, branches and needles are LOD-reduced and
quantized without enlargement, with photographic albedo under the shared PBR
lighting. Whole-tree photographic cards are no longer loaded at this entry.
Black unused atlas texels are padded with the nearest existing photographed
color (no recoloring or generated texture detail), reducing black mip bleed.
Visible trunk triangles supply body collision without becoming ground support.
Beyond 96m (Low) / 192m (High), a half-metre vertex-cluster LOD of the same
captured triangles is used; original tile-rim vertices remain. This LOD supplies
terrain queries. Near geometry is not clustered. Subpixel photographic grain
fades with screen footprint; the complete mid-scale photograph remains.

Measured Huangshan landscape and georeferenced imagery
-----------------------------------------------------
Terrain elevations: USGS SRTM, approximately 30 m native horizontal resolution,
distributed by Mapzen Terrain Tiles (public-domain USGS data; retain attribution).
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/tree/master/docs
AOI origin: 30.119 N, 118.161 E, Huangshan, China. Local datum is translated to
7 m at the cave exit without vertical exaggeration. Native elevation samples
are cropped, quantized to 0.125 m, interpolated and meshed at 12 m. This does
NOT make the source a 12 m survey, nor resolve near-ground rocks or overhangs.
The authored hiking ribbon follows that measured surface; it does not define
or deform the surrounding hills. No noise-based or route-distance mountains.
Source tile URLs and SHA-256 hashes: src/xianxia-terrain-data.json.
Reproducer: scripts/import-xianxia-terrain.py EXISTING_DOWNLOAD_CACHE_DIRECTORY
(Python dependencies: numpy, Pillow, requests).

Mature pine canopy
-----------------
Mature pine canopy LODs — Pine Tree 01, Poly Haven, CC0.
Photography: Rob Tuytel; modeling: Rico Cilliers.
https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01 · https://polyhaven.com/license
Variant B retains authored metre-scale trunk and branch geometry. This is a photo-textured tree model, not a terrain scan. Offline needle LODs preserve projected foliage coverage by widening individual disconnected needles without scaling the tree or its branches. Original vertex-color lighting is removed; both LODs reuse the near model's photographic materials under the host's shared HDR/ACES lighting. The far GLB contains geometry only. No mesh/texture codec, WASM, worker or provider request is introduced.
The v2 GLBs pad near-black empty areas of the opaque twig atlas from adjacent photographed texels before generating mipmaps. Colored source texels are retained; mip levels no longer mix unused black atlas space into fine needles. Runtime needle albedo is compensated once per shared material, with no emissive glow or change to host exposure. Older GLBs remain archived but are not requested by the current loader.
The v3 mature and v1 medium LODs classify slender needle components by aspect ratio: the former radius-only method also enlarged small rounded cone scales and is no longer used. Coverage compensation measures needles only, averaged over three projection axes. The authored approximately 7.01m medium trees restore the missing middle canopy below approximately 14.9m adults and above 1.05m seedlings; whole trees are not enlarged. Authored material specular/IOR properties are retained. Four GLB LODs are transported with standard gzip and native browser decompression, without a new JavaScript codec. Older source versions are not requested.
Low mature v5 / medium v3 and High mature v7 / medium v5 replace undersampled individual needle triangles with fixed crossed photographs of a real pine shoot from the same author's original twig atlas (normalized crop x=0, y=.04, width=.235, height=.40; native241x410 pixels at1K). A connected brightness matte removes padding islands; photographed RGB is padded outside its silhouette without upscaling or synthesized detail. Clusters and axes follow original green needle geometry, not generated skeletons or camera-facing whole-tree impostors. High preserves ALL non-slender source twig surfaces, including those inside the needle UV region, rather than relying on atlas regions alone. Smaller shoot clusters use true vertex-projected axis spans rather than inflated bounding-box spans. Photographs fit uniformly inside original needle bounds, without per-corner distortion. Wood/cones retain proportions, original color and authored specular/IOR. MASK/.18 avoids stochastic alpha grain; foliage-only albedo balance does not brighten wood or change host lighting/exposure. Far files reuse near materials and retain UVs. Low models and density are unchanged. New visual/cold-load acceptance remains required.

High v8 / middle v6 native-geometry revision: the original twig atlas also receives nearest-source RGB padding in near-black void texels; source colors above the padding threshold are preserved before encoding. Authored woody forks and non-slender needle-region remnants get separate simplification budgets, then rejoin one primitive/material: no added runtime textures or draw segments. No synthetic skeleton, wood/cone widening, material color gain or specular/IOR changes. Photo sprays remain unchanged from v7/v5; the complete new frame is still pending acceptance.

Streamed mountain surface revision 3 (MTN2)
-----------------------------------
The 64 m source corridor retains the authored Coastal Cliff 04 front surfaces over continuous gentle earth; no boolean backing shells, generated rock walls or union-cut rock feet are drawn. Earth comes from valid captured support samples with isotropic filtering and original Rocky Trail photographic displacement, not an axis-aligned slope envelope or an extrusion of out-of-capture raster edges. The fixed entrance is retained. Only earth is softened at mirrored lateral rims; steep faces remain captured rock. Captured LOD simplification locks the original clipped tile borders; near/far earth also retains the same fine boundary samples. Generated earth triangles stay below 45 degrees. MTN2 adds one normalized ground-cover byte per vertex, derived from actual earth proximity and upward normals, replacing the narrow route-only material mask. Visible terrain and movement queries decode the same final triangles, including the earth surface. Versioned v3 binaries leave legacy MTN1 and v2 MTN2 URLs intact, so cached clients cannot mix old and rebuilt terrain. Existing photographic layers, scanned stones, ferns and pines are reused; no additional texture source is introduced.
