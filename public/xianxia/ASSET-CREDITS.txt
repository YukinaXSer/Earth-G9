青岚境 / Earth 2 Xianxia — material provenance
===========================================
This is an interpreted, fictional walkable landscape, NOT a digital twin of a real hiking route. Route layout, camera, placement, collisions and cave arrangement are authored. Mountain elevations come from measured Huangshan terrain, not profiles invented around the route. The background photograph is not walkable geometry. The cave body and entrances use the licensed real captures credited below, adapted and joined for this fictional journey. Visual acceptance is still pending.

Measured Huangshan landscape and georeferenced imagery
-----------------------------------------------------
Terrain elevations: USGS SRTM, approximately 30 m native horizontal resolution,
distributed by Mapzen Terrain Tiles (public-domain USGS data; retain attribution).
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/tree/master/docs
AOI origin: 30.119 N, 118.161 E, Huangshan, China. Local datum is translated to
7 m at the cave exit without vertical exaggeration. Native elevation samples
are cropped, quantized to 0.125 m, interpolated and meshed at 12 m. This does
NOT make the source a 12 m survey, nor resolve near-ground rocks or overhangs.
The authored hiking ribbon follows that measured surface; it does not define
or deform the surrounding hills. No noise-based or route-distance mountains.
Source tile URLs and SHA-256 hashes: src/xianxia-terrain-data.json.
Reproducer: scripts/import-xianxia-terrain.py EXISTING_DOWNLOAD_CACHE_DIRECTORY
(Python dependencies: numpy, Pillow, requests).

Changes: rock GLTF converted to binary GLB; mesh welding, simplification, pruning and deduplication with glTF Transform and Meshoptimizer; textures converted to embedded WebP. Separate low-quality rock GLBs use further simplification and 512 px textures. The old repeated rock-face cave and stacked entrance were removed; their source files remain for provenance. Scenery is instanced and distance-culled. Rocky trail photographs supply color, normal and displacement textures.

Pine foliage is not aggressively decimated: all three complete, detailed pine_sapling_medium variants are lit and rendered offline to 1536x2048 transparent PNG impostors. Both the original photographic alpha mask and leaf-material alpha testing are restored before rendering. Runtime needle edges use soft transparency and FXAA rather than a hard low-resolution alpha cutout. The runtime uses camera-facing instanced images, preserving the dense authored needles without millions of geometry triangles per tree. These are renders of a photo-textured model, not original photographs of whole trees. Native model dimensions are retained in tree-dimensions.json and the scene varies scale slightly. Close rocks and the walkable terrain remain actual 3D geometry.

Original capture: iPhone 12 LiDAR, Scaniverse, Iceland, 2022. Used as
models/cave-scan.glb. Adaptations: rigid orientation, uniform 6.736684x
enlargement, longitudinal clipping for the joins, small capture-hole repairs
using neighbouring photographed surface patches, decimation and embedded WebP.
The centre-floor route is measured from that captured geometry; runtime feet
intersect the actual rendered scan triangles. No procedural tunnel enclosure.

from source units, backside clipping and ground removal at the joins, mesh

Engine, original rainforest and text
------------------------------------
Three.js 0.180.0 — MIT; see /THREE-LICENSE.txt.
All original rainforest credits and licenses are preserved at /ASSET-CREDITS.txt, /FONT-LICENSE.txt and /THREE-LICENSE.txt. This module does not replace them.
Poetry quoted in the interface is public-domain classical Chinese poetry by Wang Wei, Jia Dao and Du Fu; authors and poem names are displayed beside each verse. Wind ambience is synthesized, not a field recording. Interface text and fictional location names are authored for this experience.

All assets needed at runtime are bundled locally. There is no runtime fetch to asset providers, login service, CDN, iframe or navigation-based world transition.
