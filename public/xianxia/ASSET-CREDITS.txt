青岚境 / Earth 2 Xianxia — material provenance
===========================================
This is an interpreted, fictional walkable landscape, NOT a digital twin of a real hiking route. Route layout, camera, placement, collisions and cave arrangement are authored. Close terrain uses a complete photographed landscape, with measured Huangshan elevations beyond it; neither is a profile invented around the route. The background photograph is not walkable geometry. The cave body and entrances use the licensed real captures credited below, adapted and joined for this fictional journey. Visual acceptance is still pending.

Close-scale photographed landscape
----------------------------------
The mountain-only entry now uses Coastal Cliff 04 by Poly Haven (CC0):
https://polyhaven.com/a/coastal_cliff_04
Native-scale scan surfaces are joined offline to a graded walking corridor
derived from the captured height profile and Rocky Trail photographic
displacement. Infill slopes are limited to 0.5 per axis before union
(35.3 degrees maximum combined grade); cliff silhouettes, ledges and
overhangs retain the scan's native geometry, without lateral planar caps.
Four shared near/far tiles repeat with alternating X reflection.
The two one-metre terminal bands blend the tangent between reflected blocks;
the remaining captured interior is not enlarged. The visible baked triangles
also provide foot height and wall collision. This is an authored composite,
not a continuous one-kilometre survey. No aerial geometry is loaded by this entry.
The earlier aerial terrain described below is retained only for legacy code.

Mountain walking surfaces use Forest Floor by Poly Haven, CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor
Bare-rock detail uses Rock043L by ambientCG, CC0:
https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock043L
Full photographic layers repeat at 2.14m and 1.8m; native detail crops at .2675m
and .225m add grain. Original albedo, normal and roughness are repacked into
precompressed GPU texture blocks; source dimensions, crop bounds, format,
licenses and checksums are listed in surface/manifest.json. Scattered rocks
and ferns retain their captured scale and are grounded against visible tiles.
Entry vegetation includes native-scale Pine Sapling Small by Poly Haven, CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_small
Only one original approximately 1.05m seedling is retained, with quantized
geometry and compressed photographic albedo/alpha; it is not enlarged.
The taller pines use an original approximately 7.01m Pine Sapling Medium model:
https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_medium
Its native three-dimensional trunk, branches and needles are LOD-reduced and
quantized without enlargement, with photographic albedo under the shared PBR
lighting. Whole-tree photographic cards are no longer loaded at this entry.
Black unused atlas texels are padded with the nearest existing photographed
color (no recoloring or generated texture detail), reducing black mip bleed.
Visible trunk triangles supply body collision without becoming ground support.
Beyond 96m (Low) / 192m (High), a half-metre vertex-cluster LOD of the same
captured triangles is used; original tile-rim vertices remain. This LOD supplies
terrain queries. Near geometry is not clustered. Subpixel photographic grain
fades with screen footprint; the complete mid-scale photograph remains.

Measured Huangshan landscape and georeferenced imagery
-----------------------------------------------------
Terrain elevations: USGS SRTM, approximately 30 m native horizontal resolution,
distributed by Mapzen Terrain Tiles (public-domain USGS data; retain attribution).
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/tree/master/docs
AOI origin: 30.119 N, 118.161 E, Huangshan, China. Local datum is translated to
7 m at the cave exit without vertical exaggeration. Native elevation samples
are cropped, quantized to 0.125 m, interpolated and meshed at 12 m. This does
NOT make the source a 12 m survey, nor resolve near-ground rocks or overhangs.
The authored hiking ribbon follows that measured surface; it does not define
or deform the surrounding hills. No noise-based or route-distance mountains.
Source tile URLs and SHA-256 hashes: src/xianxia-terrain-data.json.

https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ce71dc2848984acfb11d8002a48a6043
Captured from 46 photographs using 3DF Zephyr, including an actual arched
entrance, forested approach, floor, interior walls and ceiling. Used as
models/cave-scan.glb. Replaces the separate LiDAR body, Hohlenstein-Stadel
entrance and subsequently rejected Mossy Sandstone Cave shelter; those are
not used in the runtime. Source: about 698,000 triangles and nine native
1024-pixel texture tiles. Native normalized units are uniformly scaled by
0.04 for a human-height walkable passage; no claim of surveyed physical size.
The captured arm is duplicated and opposed at its photographed interior,
with a 1 m overlap. A single shared rigid tilt connects the rainforest datum
to the mountain datum 7 m higher, preserving the source proportions. No
independent stretching, generated tunnel or decorative stacked mouth rocks.
Adaptations: rigid orientation, front/back clipping with interpolated UVs,
conservative simplification and embedded quality-95 WebP without upscaling.
The floor route and headroom are derived from captured triangles. Runtime
feet and ceiling collision use the final rendered mesh, not an invented floor.
Source matrices and enclosed lighting range: src/xianxia-cave-captures.json.

Streamed mountain surface revision 3 (MTN2)
-----------------------------------
The 64 m source corridor retains the authored Coastal Cliff 04 front surfaces over continuous gentle earth; no boolean backing shells, generated rock walls or union-cut rock feet are drawn. Earth comes from valid captured support samples with isotropic filtering and original Rocky Trail photographic displacement, not an axis-aligned slope envelope or an extrusion of out-of-capture raster edges. The fixed entrance is retained. Only earth is softened at mirrored lateral rims; steep faces remain captured rock. Captured LOD simplification locks the original clipped tile borders; near/far earth also retains the same fine boundary samples. Generated earth triangles stay below 45 degrees. MTN2 adds one normalized ground-cover byte per vertex, derived from actual earth proximity and upward normals, replacing the narrow route-only material mask. Visible terrain and movement queries decode the same final triangles, including the earth surface. Versioned v3 binaries leave legacy MTN1 and v2 MTN2 URLs intact, so cached clients cannot mix old and rebuilt terrain. Existing photographic layers, scanned stones, ferns and pines are reused; no additional texture source is introduced.
