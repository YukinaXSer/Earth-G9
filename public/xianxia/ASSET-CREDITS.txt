青岚境 / Earth 2 Xianxia — material provenance
===========================================
This is an interpreted, fictional walkable landscape, NOT a survey or digital twin of Huangshan or Wudang. Route layout, camera, placement, collisions and cave arrangement are authored. The background photograph is not walkable geometry. Photographed material displacement adds local trail relief; it does not represent surveyed mountain elevations. The cave uses repeated scanned rock surfaces, not a measured cave scan.

Changes: rock GLTF converted to binary GLB; mesh welding, simplification, pruning and deduplication with glTF Transform and Meshoptimizer; textures converted to embedded WebP. Separate low-quality rock GLBs use further simplification and 512 px textures. Rock surfaces are repositioned, scaled and rotated to construct a fictional walkable cave and mountain trails. The cave wall scan is rebased to keep its solid side outside the collision corridor. Scenery is instanced and distance-culled. Rocky trail photographs supply color, normal and displacement textures.

Pine foliage is not aggressively decimated: all three complete, detailed pine_sapling_medium variants are lit and rendered offline to 768x1024 transparent PNG impostors. The original photographic alpha mask is restored before rendering. The runtime uses camera-facing instanced images, preserving the dense authored needles without millions of geometry triangles per tree. These are renders of a photo-textured model, not original photographs of whole trees. Native model dimensions are retained in tree-dimensions.json and the scene varies scale slightly. Close rocks and the walkable terrain remain actual 3D geometry.

A continuous cave backing uses rock_wall_02 photographed diffuse, normal, roughness and displacement maps, with relief sampled into the walkable tunnel enclosure. Authored tunnel cross-sections and bending are not a scan of an actual cave. This backing closes gaps behind individual scan meshes; the entrance massing uses the coastal_cliff_02 scan. The cave must not be represented as a survey or digital twin of a real location.

Engine, original rainforest and text
------------------------------------
Three.js 0.180.0 — MIT; see /THREE-LICENSE.txt.
All original rainforest credits and licenses are preserved at /ASSET-CREDITS.txt, /FONT-LICENSE.txt and /THREE-LICENSE.txt. This module does not replace them.
