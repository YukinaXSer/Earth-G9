青岚境 / Earth 2 Xianxia — material provenance
===========================================
This is an interpreted, fictional walkable landscape, NOT a digital twin of a real hiking route. Route layout, camera, placement, collisions and cave arrangement are authored. Close terrain uses a complete photographed landscape, with measured Huangshan elevations beyond it; neither is a profile invented around the route. The background photograph is not walkable geometry. The cave body and entrances use the licensed real captures credited below, adapted and joined for this fictional journey. Visual acceptance is still pending.

Close-scale photographed landscape
----------------------------------
"Mountain terrain - Haytor Dartmoor National Park" by Chris Bibb / chrisbibb.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/6d15587128a5451d9e29d6b50061045e
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Original capture: 300 oblique aerial photographs, approximately 50 m above
the outcrops, 4K texture. The WHOLE captured terrain is used as actual 3D
geometry, not as a photograph on an invented slope or a flat support plane.
File: models/terrain-scan.glb. Changes: rigid translation, uniform 4.5x
enlargement in all axes, clipping at the cave join, UV-preserving boundary
triangulation, smooth normals, decimation and embedded WebP. Additional
licensed near-surface photographic normal/luminance detail is applied without
inventing terrain displacement. Player feet intersect the final visible
triangles; a 12 m proxy is used only to mosaic the distant measured terrain
around the capture boundary, not to replace the foreground scan geometry.
Source transformation, uniform scale and original SHA-256 are retained in
src/xianxia-foreground-data.json. The English source terrain is deliberately
reinterpreted in this fictional Chinese-themed composite, not relabelled as
a survey of Huangshan. Public source copy: ScanSceneverse, credited below.

Measured Huangshan landscape and georeferenced imagery
-----------------------------------------------------
Terrain elevations: USGS SRTM, approximately 30 m native horizontal resolution,
distributed by Mapzen Terrain Tiles (public-domain USGS data; retain attribution).
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/tree/master/docs
AOI origin: 30.119 N, 118.161 E, Huangshan, China. Local datum is translated to
7 m at the cave exit without vertical exaggeration. Native elevation samples
are cropped, quantized to 0.125 m, interpolated and meshed at 12 m. This does
NOT make the source a 12 m survey, nor resolve near-ground rocks or overhangs.
The authored hiking ribbon follows that measured surface; it does not define
or deform the surrounding hills. No noise-based or route-distance mountains.
Source tile URLs and SHA-256 hashes: src/xianxia-terrain-data.json.
Reproducer: scripts/import-xianxia-terrain.py EXISTING_DOWNLOAD_CACHE_DIRECTORY
(Python dependencies: numpy, Pillow, requests).

Changes: rock GLTF converted to binary GLB; mesh welding, simplification, pruning and deduplication with glTF Transform and Meshoptimizer; textures converted to embedded WebP. Separate low-quality rock GLBs use further simplification and 512 px textures. The old repeated rock-face cave and stacked entrance were removed; their source files remain for provenance. Scenery is instanced and distance-culled. Rocky trail photographs supply color, normal and displacement textures.

Pine foliage is not aggressively decimated: all three complete, detailed pine_sapling_medium variants are lit and rendered offline to 1536x2048 transparent PNG impostors. Both the original photographic alpha mask and leaf-material alpha testing are restored before rendering. Runtime needle edges use soft transparency and FXAA rather than a hard low-resolution alpha cutout. The runtime uses camera-facing instanced images, preserving the dense authored needles without millions of geometry triangles per tree. These are renders of a photo-textured model, not original photographs of whole trees. Native model dimensions are retained in tree-dimensions.json and the scene varies scale slightly. Close rocks and the walkable terrain remain actual 3D geometry.

Captured cave geometry (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
"Sea Cave 3" by b_nealie.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ce71dc2848984acfb11d8002a48a6043
Captured from 46 photographs using 3DF Zephyr, including an actual arched
entrance, forested approach, floor, interior walls and ceiling. Used as
models/cave-scan.glb. Replaces the separate LiDAR body, Hohlenstein-Stadel
entrance and subsequently rejected Mossy Sandstone Cave shelter; those are
not used in the runtime. Source: about 698,000 triangles and nine native
1024-pixel texture tiles. Native normalized units are uniformly scaled by
0.04 for a human-height walkable passage; no claim of surveyed physical size.
The captured arm is duplicated and opposed at its photographed interior,
with a 1 m overlap. A single shared rigid tilt connects the rainforest datum
to the mountain datum 7 m higher, preserving the source proportions. No
independent stretching, generated tunnel or decorative stacked mouth rocks.
Adaptations: rigid orientation, front/back clipping with interpolated UVs,
conservative simplification and embedded quality-95 WebP without upscaling.
The floor route and headroom are derived from captured triangles. Runtime
feet and ceiling collision use the final rendered mesh, not an invented floor.
Source matrices and enclosed lighting range: src/xianxia-cave-captures.json.
Original GLB SHA-256: 44eef15244164d8503361b51dda69c3954cc1d784dfc9afbb88832cf3d72727e

Walking-distance surface detail uses the photographed 4K diffuse and
displacement of Poly Haven's rock_wall_02 (CC0, native physical tile 2 x 2 m):
https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_wall_02
Files: textures/cave-detail-color.webp and cave-detail-height.webp.
Triplanar photographic albedo replaces the blurry baked wall/roof color;
the captured floor and dark crevice occlusion remain. Derivative bump adds
real photographed surface relief without changing the captured cave shape.
Bump strength is an authored appearance setting, not measured cave relief.
No synthetic noise, generative upscaling or displacement of the cave mesh.
The source 16-bit height is quantized to 8-bit lossless WebP; color is quality
95 WebP. The cropped floor edge fades over 1.5 m onto the demo's existing
forest ground, which is graded to the captured boundary heights. Only that
demo instance is graded; the rainforest's source terrain is unchanged.
Diffuse download SHA-256: 08033b35eec27a9f806881d6fedd18c55433ef0130092f851b3c019e57989a68
Height download SHA-256: 24986dff8c35a58d194c5866762abd3f6210b7a7519dca12bec3d6da87ca9678

Engine, original rainforest and text
------------------------------------
Three.js 0.180.0 — MIT; see /THREE-LICENSE.txt.
All original rainforest credits and licenses are preserved at /ASSET-CREDITS.txt, /FONT-LICENSE.txt and /THREE-LICENSE.txt. This module does not replace them.
Poetry quoted in the interface is public-domain classical Chinese poetry by Wang Wei, Jia Dao and Du Fu; authors and poem names are displayed beside each verse. Wind ambience is synthesized, not a field recording. Interface text and fictional location names are authored for this experience.
